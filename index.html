Strategic Insights & Technical Findings

Nordic Model Supremacy

Finland, Denmark, and Iceland lead the world due to universal healthcare, low income inequality, and high trust.

Economic vs Social Elasticity

GDP per capita correlates strongly (+0.79), but social support (+0.76) prevents catastrophic drops during recessions.

The Corruption Drag

Perceived corruption correlates negatively (-0.43) with subjective wellbeing, eroding institutional confidence.

Generosity Paradox

Financial generosity shows weak standalone correlation (+0.07), serving as an outcome rather than a primary driver of stability.
